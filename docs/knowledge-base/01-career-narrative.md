---

## Why AI Product Management Specifically

The interesting problem in AI right now isn't building a smarter model — it's the product layer around it. Where do you ground the model? Where do you keep a human in the loop? How do you build trust with users who'll abandon the tool the first time it hallucinates?

I kept solving that problem at Landeed — the AITSR pivot (away from "intelligent analyst" toward "reliable copilot") is the clearest example. The lawyers didn't want the AI to reason over documents. They wanted it to extract reliably and let them edit fast. That insight came from being forward-deployed, not from a product spec.

That problem — making AI dependable enough to put in front of users who can't tolerate it being wrong — is what I want to go deeper on.
